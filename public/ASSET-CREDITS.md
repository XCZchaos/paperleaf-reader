# Paperleaf visual assets

- Paperleaf book/leaf icon: generated specifically for this application; no third-party logo. PNG and multi-resolution ICO derived from the original.
- Library desk photograph: Ilya Semenov, Unsplash, https://unsplash.com/photos/a-green-desk-lamp-in-a-library-B8HRcng8Ibg . Downloaded November 4, 2026 under https://unsplash.com/license . Locally resized and encoded as WebP. Attribution is also displayed in the library hero.
- Interface icons: Lucide, ISC license (dependency attribution retained).

- Argument study typography: Lora 5.3.0 and IBM Plex Sans 5.3.0, distributed via @fontsource under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Local WOFF2 files and full licenses are in public/fonts/. Exported HTML embeds both fonts and licenses for offline use.
